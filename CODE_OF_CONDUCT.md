# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults and personal
attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer, [@hikmethankolay](https://github.com/hikmethankolay),
privately through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove content or block
participants who break these rules.
